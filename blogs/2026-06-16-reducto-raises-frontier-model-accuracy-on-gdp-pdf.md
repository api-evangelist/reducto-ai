Reducto tested whether pairing frontier models with structured document parsing improves performance on the GDP.pdf benchmark, where frontier models struggle with complex documents (Claude Fable 5 scored only 30% on whole-task success). Adding Reducto's structured parse alongside raw PDFs lifted macro accuracy +9pp, from 21.7% to 30.7% — roughly 40% more fully-correct tasks. The largest gains came from reasoning-heavy models (Opus 4.8 +11pp, GPT-5.5 +10pp), and engineering documents saw the biggest lift at +16.7pp macro accuracy.
